## Reading these results

- The pendulum measures g from time (the period) and the spoken string length, so it does not depend on a spatial scale. It is the reliable experiment on an ordinary phone.
- Free fall needs a spatial scale and a fast camera. The accepted free-fall runs were off by -12.1 %; FrameLab flagged them with a plausibility warning (G4) but did not refuse them, because only one scale (the spoken ball diameter) was available.
- G2 can only check the scale when two scales exist (a ruler of known length and the ball diameter). A trap without a ruler of known length cannot be caught by G2.
- Runs marked as analysed before a fix were not re-run to save API credits.

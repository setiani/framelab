# How to record an experiment for FrameLab

## All experiments
- Keep the phone **completely still**: use a tripod or lean it on a stack of books. Never hand-hold.
- Point the camera **perpendicular** to the plane of motion, about 1–1.5 m away.
- Put a ruler or measuring tape **in the same plane** as the motion (e.g. tape it to the wall
  directly behind the falling ball or the swinging bob).
- Use a plain, contrasting background (white wall, orange/red ball) and bright light from the front.
- Turn off video stabilisation if your phone allows it.
- Record 10–20 seconds. At the start, **say the parameters out loud**, e.g.
  *"Tinggi jatuh satu meter, diameter bola enam koma tujuh senti."*
  You can also state a hypothesis: *"Saya kira periodenya satu setengah detik."*

## Free fall
- Use **slow motion** (120 or 240 fps) if your phone has it.
- Use a solid, slightly heavy ball (tennis or rubber ball), not a ping-pong ball.
- Drop height 0.8–1.5 m.
- Some phones save slow-motion videos at 30 fps playback speed. If so, choose the real
  capture fps (120 or 240) in the upload form.

## Pendulum
- 30 or 60 fps is enough.
- Use a small, heavy bob (a large nut or a rubber ball) on a thin string.
- Keep the swing small (less than about 15°) and record at least 10 swings.
- Measure the length **from the pivot to the centre of the bob**, and say it out loud.

## Safety
Heights up to 2 m only. Use household objects; no glass, fire or mains electricity.
Children should experiment with an adult. When filming children, show hands and apparatus only.

## Ground truth (for the benchmark)
Write down the drop height, string length, ball diameter, ruler length, the capture fps, and,
for pendulums, the stopwatch time of 10 swings (3 trials). Add it to `samples/ground_truth.json`.
